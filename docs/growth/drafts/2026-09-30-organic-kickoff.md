## 1) Reddit — 3 post drafts

### R1 — r/nosurf
**Why it fits:** Core ICP (phone addiction, intentional friction). Honesty > hard sell.

**Title:** I kept “just checking” my phone until half the day was gone — so I’m building something that locks apps until I finish what I promised myself

**Body:**
I’m not looking for a diagnosis. I just open Instagram or Reddit “for a second,” and somehow it’s 40 minutes later and the thing I meant to do never happened.

I already tried Screen Time, blockers I could dismiss, and raw willpower. I still bypassed myself.

What I actually needed: **apps stay locked until today’s habits are done.**

So I’ve been building **KEPT** (Keep Every Promise Today) for Android:

- Pick 1–4 daily habits + a lock window.
- During that window, other apps stay locked until those are done (phone, messages, maps, camera, settings stay up; you can add a short exceptions list).
- Break lock exists if life happens — countdown + a real cost, not a fake “are you sure?”
- Sprig, a little creature, evolves as your streak grows.

Not a parent-control app. No dashboard for someone else. No account. No “we watch what you do inside apps.” It’s a lock *you* set so you keep the promise you already made yourself.

Building / private testing — not on Play yet. Happy to put you on the list / share when open testing starts.

If you’ve tried blockers before: what made you uninstall?

## 3) Reply kit — “How do I stop doomscrolling?” (one-pager)

Replies in existing threads only. Empathy first. One useful idea even if they never try KEPT. Soft waitlist language. Never parental-control / under-13 / Families / medical / unbypassable / Play download / iOS.

### Opening moves (pick one)
- “Same. Knowing it’s bad doesn’t help when the app is still one tap away.”
- “I didn’t need another dashboard. I needed the distracting apps unavailable until I did the thing I already promised.”
- “If Screen Time / willpower fixed it, we wouldn’t all still be here.”

### Core advice block
1. Pick **1–3** tiny daily promises (not ten).
2. During a set window, **lock scroll apps until those are done** (lock-until-done, not block-forever).
3. Keep a real **escape hatch** (calls/emergency always; Break lock with honest cost) so you don’t rage-uninstall.
4. Make the streak *visible* — a creature / form unlock beats a gray number for a lot of people.

### If they ask “what app?”
“I’ve been building KEPT for Android — locks other apps until today’s habits are done, Sprig evolves with the streak, no account / not a parent dashboard. Building / private testing — not on Play yet; happy to put you on the list / share when open testing starts. Pattern above works with other blockers too.”

### Objections
- **Too harsh:** “Harsh with no escape = prison = uninstall. Fair version is default lock + rare costly unlock + habits you chose.”
- **Parents / kids:** “Tools people set for themselves. No parent dashboard, no Families-style monitoring, we don’t watch what you do inside apps.”
- **Just delete social:** “Works for some. For others it creeps back. Friction + a small daily win often outlasts white-knuckle deletion.”
- **Is it foolproof?:** “No — and I won’t claim that. Honest friction + streak consequences, not supervised-mode lockdown.”
