## After they say yes

1. Add their Gmail to Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → Testers → "KEPT internal testers".
2. Send them the closed-test join link from that same screen.
3. Ask them to open the link on their phone, accept, and install.
4. Reply on [#69](https://github.com/anujabbi/kept/issues/69) (or tell App Manager) with how many are opted in so the 14-day clock can be recorded once Google finishes reviewing the track.
