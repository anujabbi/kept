# Zero-budget launch & growth (KEPT)

Constraint: **no paid ads, no paid influencers, no paid tools beyond what we
already have.** Creativity over spend.

## Product one-liner

KEPT locks every app on your phone until today's habits are done. Sprig evolves
with your streak. No account, no parent dashboard, privacy-first.

## North-star (v1)

- **Install → day-7 streak** rate among people who finish onboarding.
- Secondary: Play listing conversion (impressions → installs) once listed.

## ICP

Primary: people roughly **high school through 30s** who want less phone
addiction and more follow-through, but fail with willpower alone.
Secondary: someone who recommends KEPT (friend, partner, parent) — install is
still on the user's phone; there is no parental-control surface.

## Channels ($0)

| Channel | Play | Why it fits KEPT |
|---------|------|------------------|
| Own network seed | Week 1 | 20–50 people from friend/family/work circles; ask for honest Day-3 + Day-7 feedback |
| Sprig share cards | Ongoing | Built-in PNG share is free UGC; prompt after form unlocks |
| Reddit / Discord organic | Ongoing | Answer screen-time / habit / phone-addiction threads; never spam; lead with the problem |
| Short vertical video | Ongoing | Record lock → habit → unlock → Sprig cheer on a real device; post from personal accounts |
| Product Hunt / indie Android lists | At open listing | One honest launch post with demo video |
| Soft institutional intros (optional) | Later | Counselors, campuses, wellness groups — only if a warm intro exists; no cold spam |

## Launch sequence

1. **Private closed testing** — friends + 10–20 volunteers; fix uninstall-driving bugs.
2. **Create Play Console + listing ready** — privacy URL live, demo video, Data safety, screenshots. *(Play Console not created yet.)*
3. **Open testing** — share Sprig cards + organic posts; watch Day-7 streak.
4. **Production** — only after hollow-day / lock reliability looks clean in PostHog.

## Experiments backlog (each becomes `type:experiment` on the board)

- E1: Onboarding copy A/B (promise framing vs lock framing)
- E2: Share-card prompt timing (form unlock vs day complete)
- E3: Recommender one-pager (for friends/parents) vs self-serve Reddit posts
- E4: Wellness / campus soft-intro template (opt-in only, no spam lists)

## What we will not do

- Buy installs or fake reviews
- Collect emails without a clear reason (no account today)
- Promise parental controls we do not ship
