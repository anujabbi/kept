## App name (max 30)

KEPT: Keep Every Promise Today

## Short description (max 80)

Apps stay locked until today's habits are done. Keep the streak, grow Sprig.

## Full description (max 4000)

Willpower loses to an infinite feed. KEPT changes the deal: your phone stays locked until you keep
the promises you made to yourself today.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick one to ten daily habits: read, work out, study, stretch, whatever matters to you.
• Until they're done, every app is locked except the essentials (phone, messages, maps, camera,
  settings) and a short list of exceptions you choose.
• Mark a habit done with a tap, or with photo proof when you want to be honest with yourself.
• Finish the day and your streak grows. Hit milestones and Sprig, your little companion, evolves.
  Every form you earn stays in your gallery.
• A weekly recap shows how the week actually went.
• Real life happens: you get a few breaks a week, but not enough to make the lock meaningless.

BUILT FOR YOU, NOT FOR SOMEONE WATCHING YOU
• No account, no sign-up, no ads.
• What you create in KEPT stays on your phone.
• Not a parental-control app. You set it up for yourself.

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Usage access: lets KEPT see which app is in the foreground so it can lock it.
• Display over other apps: lets the lock screen appear on top of a locked app.
KEPT uses these only to run the lock.

Privacy policy: https://anujabbi.github.io/kept-site/privacy-policy.html
