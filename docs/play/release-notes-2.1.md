# What's new in 2.1 (en-US)

Paste the block below into Play Console → Release → Release notes → `<en-US>`. It is under
500 characters; check the count again after any edit.

```
New: weekly goals. Pick an activity and how many days a week you'll do it. The week runs Sunday to Saturday, and your phone locks only on the day you can no longer skip. Miss that day and your streak breaks, unless your weekly shield covers it.

New: night lockdown. Apps lock overnight between the times you set. Turn it on in Settings.

The "Sleep by 11" habit has been removed. Night lockdown does that job now, once you turn it on.
```
