   for a role with a card, repeated until a round finds nothing. Every role run is a fresh

   first incident above.
2. `scripts/board.sh`: `KEPT_WIP_LIMIT` (default 1) on `next planner`, plus `wip` to show

   sibling-conflict cause rather than its symptom.

   process pool, five-minute schedule, concurrency one, run-now from the UI.
4. `docs/AGENTS-RUNNER.md`: setup, run, track, manage, troubleshoot. AGENTS.md "Running the
   agents" rewritten; terminals stay as the fallback.
